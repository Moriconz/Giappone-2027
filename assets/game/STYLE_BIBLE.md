# Style-bible voxel — Tabi V2 (riusare testualmente in ogni prompt Higgsfield)

1. Voxel/cube art style, chunky oversized proportions (head ≈ 40% of body), visible cube grain.
2. Palette: warm Japan-inspired — vermilion torii red, matcha green, indigo, sand beige, soft cream; max 6 colors per asset.
3. Flat stylized lighting, single soft top-light, NO dynamic shadows, NO gradients inside faces.
4. Clean silhouette on plain background, centered, 3/4 front view unless specified.
5. Avoid: realistic fur/texture, photorealism, outlines, anti-aliased curves, Pokémon-like designs, text in image.
